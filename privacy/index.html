Privacy policy.

How Crater, Puttnik and Glidegang handle your information. Short version: we don’t run accounts or collect your personal information ourselves. The ads in the games come from Google, and online play goes through Apple.

Who we are

The games are made by Codelions LLC (“we”). Questions about this policy: assistant@codelionsllc.com.

Last updated November 22, 2026. This policy covers Crater, Puttnik and Glidegang for iPhone and this website.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress, coins, unlocked items, settings, the holes and courses you build, and your best scores are saved only on your device. We don’t receive them. Deleting a game deletes its saved data.

Share codes and QR codes for holes and courses contain only the hole or course itself, nothing about you.

What we collect

We don’t ask for your name, email, location or contacts, we don’t have accounts, and we don’t run our own analytics in the games. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads, plus diagnostic data. Google uses this under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

iOS asks your permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you’ll still see ads, but they won’t be personalized using that identifier. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Buying Remove Ads stops full-screen ads.

Game Center (Apple)

Online matches, leaderboards and achievements use Apple’s Game Center. When you play online, Apple shares your Game Center nickname and scores with the other players and on leaderboards. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy; we don’t receive your Apple Account details.

Same-Wi-Fi play

Party and same-Wi-Fi games talk directly between phones on your local network. Nothing is sent to us or to the internet.

Purchases (Apple)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; the game only learns which items you own.

Children

The games are made for a general audience. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Your choices

You can turn off tracking for ads in iOS settings, reset your advertising identifier, buy Remove Ads, play offline, or delete a game to erase its data. For anything else, email assistant@codelionsllc.com.

This website

This site is a plain static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads of its own. GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service: see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date above.
