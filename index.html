[ what they share ]

Physics first.

No off-the-shelf physics engine in any of them: each game’s world runs on rules we wrote, tuned until it feels right.

01The world reacts

In Crater the ground is the weapon: craters work on any slope, ridges become passes, and steep ground slides away.

02The same on every phone

Puttnik runs each shot 240 times a second from the tee, the flick and the hole’s seed — so a shot replays exactly, anywhere.

03Air you can read

Glidegang flies on its own energy model: diving buys speed, climbing spends it, and updrafts are the only free height.

04Make your own

Puttnik’s Hole Lab and Glidegang’s Course Lab: build it, beat it at par or with a bot, then share it as a code or QR.

05Play together

Game Center online, pass-and-play on one phone, or a whole room on the same Wi-Fi.

06Bots that play fair

Easy, Normal and Hard bots, tuned against a stand-in new player so a first race is winnable and a hard one isn’t.

Built with overcli.

Every game here is built with overcli flows, in the agent desktop app we make: one model plans each milestone, another builds it, and a third checks it with Unity’s own tests before we ever pick up a phone.
