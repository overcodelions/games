[ support ]

Need a hand?

Help with Crater, Puttnik and Glidegang. Email us at assistant@codelionsllc.com and a real person — Lionel or Owen — will get back to you.

Contact

Email assistant@codelionsllc.com. Tell us which game, which iPhone and iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Testing a TestFlight build? You can also send feedback from the TestFlight app: take a screenshot in the game and tap Share Beta Feedback.

Purchases and Remove Ads

Purchases are handled by Apple. If something you bought is missing — coins, a starter pack, or Remove Ads — open the game’s Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles all refunds; we can’t issue them ourselves.

Playing online

Online play, leaderboards and achievements use Apple’s Game Center. If online play doesn’t start, check that you’re signed in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone, then reopen the game.

Playing on the same Wi-Fi

Party and same-Wi-Fi games need permission to find nearby phones. If friends can’t see each other, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and turn the game on. Everyone must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Ads

The games show optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch them for a bonus) and occasional full-screen ads between games, never during play. Buying Remove Ads turns off the full-screen ads.

Your data and progress

Your progress is saved on your iPhone. We don’t have accounts, so there’s nothing for us to look up or reset. Deleting the game deletes its saved progress. See our privacy policy for the details.
